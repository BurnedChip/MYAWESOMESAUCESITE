SHORT BIOGRAPHY

Tyler, the Creator (born Tyler Gregory Okonma on March 6, 1991, in Hawthorne, California) is an American rapper, producer, and fashion designer. He rose to fame as the co-founder of the alternative hip-hop collective Odd Future. Over time, he evolved from raw horrorcore rap to Grammy-winning, melodic, and jazz-infused experimental albums like Flower Boy and Igor.
